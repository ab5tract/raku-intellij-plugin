Reports unit sub on anything other than MAIN.

The semicolon form of a routine declaration exists for exactly one purpose: letting a script's whole body be sub MAIN, so that command-line arguments are parsed against its signature. No other routine may be declared that way. 


unit sub helper($x);     # The unit sub syntax is only allowed for the sub MAIN

# Correct
unit sub MAIN($file, Bool :$verbose);

say "processing $file";
