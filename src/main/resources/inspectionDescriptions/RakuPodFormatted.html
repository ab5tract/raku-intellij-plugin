Renders the Pod formatting codes B<>, I<> and U<> in bold, italic and underline respectively.

This entry reports no problems. It exists here only so the styled rendering can be switched off; there is nothing to fix and nothing will ever appear in an inspection results list. Turning it off leaves the codes shown as plain text. 


=begin pod
This word is B<bold>, this one is I<italic>, and this one is U<underlined>.
=end pod

Note for maintainers: a rendering-only feature implemented as a LocalInspectionTool is a design smell — it produces text attributes as a side effect of a visit rather than reporting a problem, and it inherits inspection machinery (severity, scope, quick fixes, batch runs) that means nothing for it. An Annotator or a syntax highlighter is the fitting home; the only thing keeping it here is that inspections come with a user-facing on/off switch for free.
