Reports a regex adverb that Raku does not define.

Adverbs are checked against the known set — among them :i (ignorecase), :m (ignoremark), :g (global), :s (sigspace), :r (ratchet), :ex/:ov and the :nth family. An unrecognised one is usually a typo or an adverb carried over from another language's flavour of regex. 


/ :foo 'text' /          # Unrecognized regex modifier ':foo'

# Correct
/ :i 'text' /
