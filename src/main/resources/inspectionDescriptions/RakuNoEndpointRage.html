Reports a .. range operator with nothing on its right-hand side.

.. is an infix operator and always needs a second operand; without one Rakudo reports Missing required term after infix. An unbounded range is written with * (Whatever) as the endpoint, which Rakudo will tell you directly — Please use ..* for indefinite range — and which produces a lazy, infinite sequence. 


my @counts = 1..;     # The range operator must have a second argument

# Correct
my @counts = 1..*;    # Lazy, unbounded
