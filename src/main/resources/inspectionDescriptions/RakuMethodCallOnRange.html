Reports a method call written directly on a range endpoint.

The range operators ^ and .. bind looser than a postfix method call, so ^10.pick parses as ^(10.pick) — a range up to a random number — not as a pick from the range. Parenthesise the range to get the intended meaning. 


say ^10.pick;         # Parses as ^(10.pick)

# Correct
say (^10).pick;
